## Supported marketing direction

Use practical questions with an explicit mapping to documented reference topics:

1. **How do I work through changing electrolyte results?** Point readers toward calcium, magnesium, phosphate, bicarbonate, chloride, potassium, and sodium chapters and the free self-assessments.
2. **What should I review when a patient's micronutrient needs change?** Point toward trace-element and vitamin coverage, monitoring material, and home PN coverage. Ongoing losses and organ function are clinical context, not a claim that every specific scenario has been verified in the complete book.
3. **How do I keep monitoring, interactions, and formulation considerations organized?** Point toward the documented appendices, rather than promising an error-proof protocol.

Avoid outcome guarantees such as “prevents refeeding syndrome,” “eliminates errors,” or “improves patient outcomes.” Do not claim guideline endorsement or that the guide incorporates any particular guideline update without checking the complete text with the author.
