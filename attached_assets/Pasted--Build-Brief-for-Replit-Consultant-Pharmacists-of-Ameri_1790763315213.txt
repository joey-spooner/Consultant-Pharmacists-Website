Paste this directly into Replit as the project brief. Full background/audit lives in `spec.md` in

## What to build

A static, responsive 5-page marketing/sales site for a clinical pharmacy advisory firm, replacing
an outdated WordPress.com site. Plain HTML/CSS/JS (or a minimal static-site approach), no CMS, no
backend beyond a third-party form handler and links out to Gumroad for checkout.

## Pages (exactly these 5, no more)

1. **Home**
2. **About**
3. **Client Relationships**
4. **Webinars**
5. **Contact**

  Clinical Pharmacy"). Not a blog post, not dated.

  broken PayloadZ checkout link** — treat this as the single most important element on the page.

## Design direction

- Quietly professional and clinical, not flashy. Competent and clean, not a personality overhaul.
- Free stock photography only (clinical/healthcare themed), no custom photography/illustration.
- The two pages needing the most visual rework are Client Relationships and Webinars (both
  currently walls of text), everything else is closer to a styling pass.

## Technical requirements

- Fully static, responsive across desktop/mobile/tablet.
- Custom favicon, per-page titles + meta descriptions, Open Graph preview image, sitemap.
- No CMS, no blog, no database.
- Build here in Replit; it will be published to GitHub Pages afterward (not Replit's own hosting).

## Explicitly do NOT build

- Any attendee sign-up/registration system on the Webinars page
- Amazon Kindle/print listings or formatting
- A street address on Contact (unconfirmed)
- Social share buttons (not yet confirmed with the client, ask before adding)
- Anything beyond these 5 pages
