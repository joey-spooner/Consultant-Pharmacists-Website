## Full text

**FOREWORD**

The *Clinical Guide to Parenteral Micronutrition* is a compact yet comprehensive handbook which has a wealth of information of interest to clinicians, pharmacists, nutritionists, and nurses.

This handbook provides information in detail about micronutrients. The chemistry, dosage, pharmacokinetics and product formulations are all given in appropriate detail. The handbook consists of chapters written by well known persons practicing in the field of clinical nutrition. Chapters cover the electrolytes, trace elements, vitamins and carnitine. In addition, in this new edition, the chapters have question and answer sections to encourage self-education. Two useful sections on TPN overview (General Review; Pediatrics and Neonatal patients) have also been included. This is a very useful and well written book that should find a place in the library of clinical nutritionists.

Khursheed N. Jeejeebhoy, M.B.B.S., Ph.D., FRCP\
St. Michael's Hospital\
30 Bond Street, Toronto, ON, Canada, M5B 1W8
